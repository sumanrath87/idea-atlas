## What it measures

The percent of findings of noncompliance, identified by the state in its districts, that were corrected within one year of identification.

**Type:** Compliance indicator.

## How it is measured

Each year the state reports the findings it made in the prior year and how many were corrected within a year. Starting with the FFY 2023 report, this includes noncompliance related to indicators 4B, 9, 10, 11, 12, and 13. It is a compliance indicator with a target of 100%.

## Where to find it

The atlas does not show this indicator. Your state's yearly report includes it.

## Questions to ask

- What kinds of noncompliance did our state find in our district?
- How was it corrected, and did the correction reach the students affected?

## Beyond compliance

Correcting a finding usually means fixing the paperwork and showing the rule is now followed. Skrtic (2005) warned that this kind of correction can leave practice unchanged.

- After a correction, did anything change for the student involved?
- Does the district look for the same problem in other schools?
