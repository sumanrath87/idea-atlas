## What it measures

The percent of parents of a child receiving special education who report that schools facilitated parent involvement as a means of improving services and results for children with disabilities.

**Type:** Results indicator.

## How it is measured

Each state designs its own survey, decides what counts as a positive answer, and chooses how to reach parents. States must report how many parents responded and whether the respondents reflect the families served. Because the surveys differ, compare a state with itself over time, not with other states.

## Where to find it

On your [state's page]({base}states/), under "Results on federal indicators." The atlas shows the 2024–25 figure.

## Questions to ask

- Did our district send the parent survey, and in which languages?
- What did the district learn from the results, and what did it change?

## Beyond compliance

A survey can tell a school how families feel. It cannot make families partners. Kozleski and Thorius (2014) describe families as partners in deciding what a school works on.

- Which families answered the survey, and which were never reached?
- Where do families help decide what the school works on next?
