## What it measures

The percent of children who were evaluated within 60 days of receiving parental consent for an initial evaluation, or within the state's own timeline if it has set one.

**Type:** Compliance indicator.

## How it is measured

States count children whose parents consented to an initial evaluation during the year and report how many were evaluated on time. A few delays are allowed, for example when the parent repeatedly does not make the child available. It is a compliance indicator with a target of 100%.

## Where to find it

On your [state's page]({base}states/), under "Results on federal indicators." The atlas shows the 2024–25 figure.

## Questions to ask

- When did the evaluation clock start for my child, and when is the deadline?
- If evaluations run late in our district, why?

## Beyond compliance

An evaluation can be on time and still miss what a child needs. Skrtic (2005) warned that required procedures can become rituals that stay disconnected from practice.

- Did the evaluation include what the family and the child's teachers see every day?
- Did the results lead to changes in the classroom, or only to a label?
