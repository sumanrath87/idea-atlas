## What it measures

The percent of children referred by early intervention (IDEA Part C) before age 3, and found eligible for preschool special education, who have an IEP developed and in place by their third birthday.

**Type:** Compliance indicator.

## How it is measured

States count children referred from early intervention and report how many had an IEP in place on time. Some delays are excluded, such as when parents refused consent or the child was referred too close to the birthday. The target is 100%.

## Where to find it

On your [state's page]({base}states/), under "Results on federal indicators." The atlas shows the 2024–25 figure.

## Questions to ask

- When should the transition meeting for my toddler happen?
- Who from the school district will join it?

## Beyond compliance

Meeting the birthday deadline is required. A smooth move from home-based early intervention to school is a different achievement. Kozleski and Thorius (2014) argue that families belong at the center of that kind of change.

- Does the family help choose the preschool setting, or is it assigned?
- Do the early intervention providers and the preschool staff talk with each other?
