## What it measures

The percent of children with IEPs, age 5 and in kindergarten through age 21, who are served (A) inside a general class 80% or more of the day, (B) inside a general class less than 40% of the day, and (C) in separate schools, residential facilities, or homebound or hospital placements.

**Type:** Results indicator.

## How it is measured

States use the fall child count of educational environments. Each state sets targets, usually to raise A and lower B and C.

## Where to find it

District pages show the same three measures in "Where students learn," and state pages compare the typical district with the nation. See the [guide to least restrictive environment]({base}guides/least-restrictive-environment/).

## Questions to ask

- How does our district compare with the typical district in the state?
- For students who spend most of the day outside general classes, what would it take to change that?

## Beyond compliance

Indicator 5 counts minutes in a room. Whether a student takes part in the class is a separate question. Ferguson, Kozleski, and Smith (2001) treat inclusion as something the whole school does, rather than a placement decided for some students.

- Do general and special education teachers plan lessons together?
- Do students with IEPs in general classes say they belong there?
