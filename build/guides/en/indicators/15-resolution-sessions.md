## What it measures

The percent of hearing requests that went to resolution sessions and were resolved through resolution session settlement agreements.

**Type:** Results indicator.

## How it is measured

After a parent files a due process complaint, the district must usually hold a resolution meeting within 15 days. States report how many meetings ended in a written agreement. States that held fewer than 10 resolution meetings in a year do not have to set a baseline or targets.

## Where to find it

State pages show the number of due process complaints and hearings. See the [guide to complaints, mediation, and due process]({base}guides/dispute-resolution/).

## Questions to ask

- What issues lead to due process complaints in our state?
- Who from the district attends resolution meetings, and can they make decisions?

## Beyond compliance

A settlement ends one dispute. Skrtic (1991b) argued that schools work best for students with disabilities when staff solve problems together and with the people they serve, before disagreements harden.

- After a settlement, does the school change anything for other students?
- Does the district track what disputes are about and share what it learns?
