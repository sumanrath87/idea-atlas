## What it measures

The percent of mediations held that resulted in mediation agreements.

**Type:** Results indicator.

## How it is measured

It counts every mediation held during the year, whether or not a due process complaint was filed. States that held fewer than 10 mediations in a year do not have to set a baseline or targets.

## Where to find it

State pages show the number of mediation requests. See the [guide to complaints, mediation, and due process]({base}guides/dispute-resolution/).

## Questions to ask

- How do families in our state request mediation, and how long does it take?
- Is mediation offered early, before a formal complaint?

## Beyond compliance

Mediation keeps families and schools talking, which a hearing does not. Kozleski and Thorius (2014) make the case for families as partners in improving schools.

- Do families come away from mediation feeling heard, whatever the result?
- Could the issue have been settled earlier, in the IEP meeting?
