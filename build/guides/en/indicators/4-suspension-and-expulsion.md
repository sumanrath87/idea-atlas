## What it measures

(A) The percent of districts with a significant discrepancy in the rate of suspensions and expulsions of more than 10 days in a school year for children with IEPs. (B) The percent of districts with a significant discrepancy by race or ethnicity in those rates that also have policies, procedures, or practices that contribute to it and do not comply with IDEA.

**Type:** Results (4A) and compliance (4B) indicator.

## How it is measured

Each state defines "significant discrepancy" and chooses whether to compare districts with each other or students with IEPs with other students in the same district. 4A is a results indicator with state targets. 4B is a compliance indicator, so its target is 0%. The data lag a year.

## Where to find it

District pages show related figures from the Civil Rights Data Collection under "Discipline and safety." Those count students suspended at least once, not removals of more than 10 days, so they are not the same measure. See the [discipline guide]({base}guides/discipline/).

## Questions to ask

- Was our district identified under indicator 4 in recent years, and what did the review find?
- How many students with IEPs were removed for more than 10 days last year?

## Beyond compliance

A finding under 4B leads to a review of policies and practices. Voulgarides (2018) found that in districts cited for discipline disparities, showing compliance often became the goal while practice stayed the same.

- After a review, did anything change in how classrooms handle behavior?
- Were the students and families most affected part of the review?
